How to play

  • Drag a face — click and drag on the cube to turn that layer.
  • Drag empty space — freely rotate the whole cube in any direction.
  • Alt / Option + drag or right-drag the cube — reorient it without turning a layer.
  • View cube — click a labeled face to snap to that exact angle.
  • Keyboard — U D L R F B turn a face; Shift reverses it.
  • The cube starts scrambled. Solve it and the way in opens.
  • Stuck? Give up and it unwinds itself.
